What is the password, and what does signing in do?

It is the private key set up for you - the same text that comes after ?owner= in your nithinpuru.com owner link. Sign in once on each browser or phone; that one step:

  • shows you this page (it stays signed in on that device),
  • stops counting your own visits on nithinpuru.com and on the tools,
  • lets that device open nithinpuru.com from India.

It is kept only in that browser. Let your browser save it when it offers, and you won't have to type it again. Anyone with the password can do the same, so don't share it.
