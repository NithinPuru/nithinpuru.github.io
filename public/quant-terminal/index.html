How to read this terminal

Quant score (ring)

0–100 blend of momentum, trend, risk-adjusted return and alpha vs the S&P 500. ≥70 strong setup, 40–69 neutral, <40 weak.

Signal chips

Oversold / Overbought use Bollinger Bands and RSI. F&G is a per-stock Fear & Greed gauge. Uptrend means the 10-week average is above the 40-week.

Risk metrics

Volatility is the typical yearly swing. Max DD is the worst fall from a peak. VaR 95% is a bad-week loss you'd expect about 1 week in 20.

Market exposure

Beta 1.5 means the stock moves about 1.5× the market. Alpha is return beyond what that exposure explains.

Range

The Range selector in the header sets the look-back window for every metric and chart, from 3 months to 10 years.

Research tools

Factor Model: exposure to market, semis, momentum, low-vol and size factors, and how much of each stock's risk they explain. Regimes: a hidden Markov model that detects calm vs turbulent markets. Rotation: who is gaining or losing relative strength. Pairs: cointegrated, mean-reverting pairs.

Portfolio tools

Optimizer: exact efficient frontier, risk parity, hierarchical risk parity and Black-Litterman with your own views. Risk & Stress: VaR broken down by holding, plus how the portfolio did in real crises and under custom shocks. Backtest: walk-forward testing with trading costs.

Table view

Switch Cards / Table above the grid for a sortable screener of every metric. Tools use whichever companies are currently visible, so filter or search first to focus on a group.

Currencies

International listings (Samsung, SK Hynix, TSMC peers, Tokyo Electron…) are shown in their home currency. Returns and ratios are unaffected.
